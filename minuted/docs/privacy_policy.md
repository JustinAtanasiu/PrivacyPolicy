# Minuted — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 13 June 2026_

Minuted is built to keep your meetings private. Everything happens on your iPhone.
There is no Minuted account, no Minuted server, and your recordings, transcripts, and
summaries are never uploaded to us or anyone else.

## The short version

- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data.
- Audio, transcripts, summaries, and speaker profiles stay **on your device**.
- All transcription and AI run **locally** using Apple's on-device models — no cloud.
- There are **no accounts**, **no analytics**, and **no tracking**.
- Deleting a meeting, or the app, permanently removes its data from your device.

## Who we are

Minuted ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application that records in-person meetings and
produces transcripts and summaries entirely on your device. This policy explains how the app
handles information.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect any information.** Minuted has no backend servers and no user accounts.
We never receive your audio, transcripts, summaries, calendar data, or any identifiers.
Because nothing is sent to us, there is no Minuted-held copy of your data to access, share,
or lose.

The app's Apple App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected."**

## Information the app processes on your device

To do its job, the app works with the following data **locally on your iPhone**. This data is
stored only on your device and is never uploaded to us:

- **Audio recordings** — captured from your microphone when you start a recording, and used to
  produce a transcript. Audio is processed on-device and stored locally. You can keep or delete
  it at any time.
- **Transcripts and summaries** — generated on-device from your audio. Decisions, action items,
  and follow-up email drafts are created locally.
- **Calendar information** — with your permission, the app reads upcoming events and attendee
  names (read-only) to label your meeting, identify speakers, and draft follow-up emails. This
  is read on demand and is not copied off your device.
- **Speaker (voice) profiles** — to recognize recurring participants, the app may store voice
  characteristics on your device. These never leave your iPhone.

## On-device AI

Transcription, speaker separation, and summarization all run on your device using Apple's
on-device technologies, including Apple's Speech framework, Apple Intelligence / Foundation
Models, and an on-device speaker-diarization model. No audio or text is sent to Apple's servers,
to us, or to any third party for this processing.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Microphone** — to record meeting audio. Recording only happens after you confirm, and you
  are asked to make sure everyone present consents.
- **Calendar** — to show your meetings and read attendee names for labeling and follow-ups.
  Read-only.
- **Speech recognition** — to transcribe audio on-device.

You can grant or revoke any of these at any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security**.
Revoking a permission disables the related feature but does not delete anything already stored
on your device.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions are handled entirely by **Apple** through the App Store using StoreKit. Apple
processes your payment and manages your subscription; **we never receive your payment details**
or financial information. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage or cancel your
subscription in **iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions**.

## Sharing

Anything you share — for example a follow-up email or an exported summary — is shared only when
**you** choose to, using your iPhone's standard share options (such as Mail). At that point the
content is handled by whichever app or service you select, under that service's own terms.
Minuted does not share your data on its own.

## Data retention and deletion

Your meetings and their data live on your device for as long as you keep them. Deleting a
meeting in the app removes that meeting's audio, transcript, and summary. Deleting the app
removes all of its data from your device. Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing
for you to request, export, or delete from us.

## Children

Minuted is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly process data from
children. As the app collects no data, no such data is sent to us in any case.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and post the revised
version here. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
