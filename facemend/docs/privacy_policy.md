# FaceMend — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

FaceMend edits your photos on your device. Your photos never leave your phone. There is no
FaceMend account, no FaceMend server, and nothing you edit is uploaded to us or anyone else.

## The short version

- We do **not** collect, store, or transmit your personal data.
- Your photos and edits stay **on your device**.
- All editing and AI (retouch, reshape, background, relight, hair color, enhance) runs
  **locally** on your iPhone. No cloud.
- There are **no accounts**, **no analytics**, and **no tracking**.
- Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

## Who we are

FaceMend ("the app", "we", "us") is an iOS application that retouches and enhances photos
entirely on your device. This policy explains how the app handles information.

## Information we collect

**We do not collect any information.** FaceMend has no backend servers and no user accounts.
We never receive your photos, your edits, or any identifiers. The app contains no analytics,
no advertising SDK, and no crash-reporting service. Because nothing is sent to us, there is
no FaceMend-held copy of your data to access, share, or lose.

The app's Apple App Store privacy label is **"Data Not Collected."**

## Your photos

To do its job, the app works with your photos **locally on your iPhone**:

- **Choosing a photo.** You pick photos with the system photo picker. FaceMend does not ask
  for access to your whole photo library and cannot browse it. You choose exactly which
  photos the app can see.
- **Editing.** Every edit (retouch, skin smoothing, blemish removal, teeth whitening,
  reshape, sculpt, background remove or swap, relight, hair color, AI enhance, filters)
  is processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.
- **Saving.** When you save an edited photo, the app asks only for permission to **add**
  photos to your library, never to read it.
- **Location data.** FaceMend removes location (GPS) data from the photos it exports.

## On-device AI

All AI features run on your device using Apple's on-device frameworks and bundled machine
learning models. No photo and no data derived from a photo is sent to Apple's servers, to
us, or to any third party for this processing.

One model (used by the object Eraser) is too large to ship inside the app. The first time
you use the Eraser, Apple delivers that model file from the App Store to your device as an
on-demand resource. This is a one-way download to your phone: no photo and no personal data
is sent anywhere as part of it.

## Permissions the app requests

- **Add to Photos** - to save your edited photos to your library. Save-only; the app does
  not request read access to your library (photo selection goes through the system picker,
  which shares only the photos you pick).

You can review permissions at any time in **iOS Settings → Privacy & Security**.

## Purchases and subscriptions

Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled entirely by **Apple** through the App
Store using StoreKit. Apple processes your payment and manages your subscription; **we never
receive your payment details** or financial information. Your purchase entitlement is
checked on your device. Apple's handling of your purchase is governed by
[Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). You can manage or cancel
your subscription in **iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions**.

## Sharing

An edited photo leaves your device only when **you** choose to share or save it, using your
iPhone's standard share options. At that point the photo is handled by whichever app or
service you select, under that service's own terms. FaceMend does not share your photos on
its own.

## Data retention and deletion

Your projects and edits live on your device for as long as you keep them. Deleting a project
in the app removes its data. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.
Because we hold none of your data, there is nothing for you to request, export, or delete
from us.

## Children

FaceMend is a general-audience photo editor and is not directed to children under 13. As the
app collects no data, no data from children is sent to us in any case.

## FaceMend on Android (Google Play)

Everything above about how FaceMend treats your photos applies to the Android app too. The
differences are only in which platform services it uses:

- **On-device processing.** Every edit runs on your phone. No photo, and no data derived
  from a photo, is sent to us, to Google or to anyone else for processing.
- **Choosing photos and videos.** You pick them with the Android system Photo Picker, which
  shares only the items you select. FaceMend cannot browse your gallery.
- **Saving.** Edited photos and videos are saved to your device gallery. On Android 10 and
  later this needs no permission. Location (GPS) data is removed from what the app exports.
- **Downloaded models.** The optional Eraser model (about 200 MB) and the depth model used
  by Relight are not inside the app download. The first time you use one of those features,
  Google Play delivers the model to your device (Play Asset Delivery). This is a one-way
  download to your phone: no photo and no personal data is sent as part of it.
- **Purchases and subscriptions.** Subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are handled
  entirely by **Google Play** (Google Play Billing). Google processes your payment and
  manages your subscription; **we never receive your payment details**. Your purchase
  entitlement is checked on your device. Google's handling of your purchase is governed by
  [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). You can manage or cancel
  a subscription in the **Google Play app → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions**.
- **Ratings prompt.** The app may show Google Play's in-app review prompt. Any rating or
  review you leave goes to Google Play, not to us.
- **No account, no analytics, no ads.** The Android app has no FaceMend account, no
  analytics, no advertising SDK or advertising ID, and no crash-reporting service. Its
  Google Play data safety section says no data is collected or shared.

**Permissions on Android:**

- **Storage (write), Android 9 and older only** - to save your edited photos to the
  gallery. Newer Android versions save without it.
- **Detect screen capture, Android 14 and later** - so the app is told when you take a
  screenshot of it and can show a short tip about saving the full-quality photo. The
  screenshot itself is never read, recorded or sent anywhere.

You can review and change permissions at any time in Android **Settings → Apps → FaceMend
→ Permissions**.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version of FaceMend ever changes how it handles your photos (for example, if a
feature ever needed to process a photo off your device), this policy and the in-app claims
will be updated to say so plainly before that feature ships, and the date at the top of this
page will change. As of this version, all processing is on your device.
